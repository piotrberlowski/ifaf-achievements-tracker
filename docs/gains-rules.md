# GAINS and record rules

## Award tracks

A score belongs to one round type. The band it earns is spent on that round’s track.

| Track | Round types |
| --- | --- |
| Indoor | Flint, IFAA Indoor |
| Animal | Standard Big Game, UAR, 3-D Standard, 3-D Hunting |
| Field | Field, Hunter, Marked Animal, Expert Field, International |

The award key is one person, one age division, one gender, one bow style, and one track. A Field Gold and a Hunter Gold are one Gold pin on the Field track.

## Classification

Bands are 90%, 80%, 70%, and 50% of the IFAF record for that round type, bow style, age division, and gender. They are Star, Gold, Silver, and Bronze. An integer score clears a cutoff when it is greater than or equal to it.

Bronze and Silver are strict bands. A score is classified into exactly one band: the highest cutoff it clears. A Gold score is not a Silver score and is not a Bronze score. It does not count toward a Silver or Bronze pin. The Star exception below is the only case where a higher band also counts as Gold.

The score is classified once, against the record in force on the day it was shot. A record set later does not change that band.

## Pins

A pin needs two scores in the same band. One qualifying score stays unpaired and is shown as progress. Scores carry across calendar years inside the same class and track. A class change starts a new career.

Each pin is handed out once per bow style, age division, gender, and track: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Hotshot, and Star.

HOTSHOT is eight scores on that track that classify as Gold. STAR is two scores at 90% or better, and it is handed out only after HOTSHOT. Until the Star pin can be handed out, a 90% score qualifies as Gold and counts toward the Gold pin and toward Hotshot.

## Records

A record is the best approved score for one round type, one bow style, one age division, and one gender. A new score replaces it only when it is strictly higher. A tie leaves the holder and the cutoffs unchanged. The tied score is kept and can still classify for GAINS.

A new record sets the cutoffs for scores shot on or after that day.
